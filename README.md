# OSINT-Tools-Finland
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bc/Flag_of_Finland.svg" alt="Finland_FLAG"/>
A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Finland. Most of the resources and tools has versions in Finnish and Swedish, many also in English. Basic company data and the electronic trade register extract are free in PRH's Virre service, but beneficial-owner data is not public: the trade register only shows whether a beneficial-owner declaration has been filed. Traficom charges for vehicle and boat owner and history data, while aircraft owner names are free. You can request property ownership details from the National Land Survey (Maanmittauslaitos) by phone free of charge for up to ten properties a year; its e-service requires strong authentication for certificates of title. The sales-price section of Asuntojen hintatiedot has been closed. Enter names with Finnish letters (ä, ö, å).

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [StatFin (Statistics Finland database)](https://pxdata.stat.fi/PxWeb/pxweb/fi/StatFin/) — Statistics Finland's main database of official statistical tables, with downloads and an API. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Suomen Pankki — Tilastot (Bank of Finland — Statistics)](https://www.suomenpankki.fi/fi/tilastot/) — central bank statistics on interest rates, loans and deposits, the balance of payments, investment funds and securities. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Finlex](https://www.finlex.fi/fi) — Ministry of Justice's free legal database: consolidated legislation, the Statute Book, decisions of Finnish courts over several decades, government bills and treaties; open data available. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Tuomioistuimet.fi — Ratkaisut (Courts of Finland — Decisions)](https://www.tuomioistuimet.fi/tuomioistuinten-ratkaisut/) — precedents of the Supreme Court and the Supreme Administrative Court, plus selected decisions of courts of appeal, administrative courts, the Market Court, the Labour Court and the Insurance Court. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Virallinen lehti (Official Gazette)](https://www.virallinenlehti.fi/fi/journal/list) — official gazette for legally required notices such as public summons, bankruptcy and debt-restructuring notices and company announcements; list of published issues. *Lang: FI, SV.*
- [Tutkihallintoa.fi (Explore Public Administration)](https://www.tutkihallintoa.fi/) — State Treasury service on public-sector organisations and finances: municipal and joint-municipal financial statements, budgets and key indicators. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Avoimuusrekisteri (Transparency Register)](https://www.avoimuusrekisteri.fi/) — National Audit Office register of lobbying aimed at Parliament and ministries: registered lobbyists, activity reports by target and topic; open data and API. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*

## Legal Entities
- [YTJ — Yritys- ja yhteisötietojärjestelmä (Business Information System)](https://tietopalvelu.ytj.fi/) — joint PRH and Tax Administration search of all registered companies and organisations: business ID (Y-tunnus), name history, address, line of business and entries in the tax registers (VAT, employer, prepayment). Free. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Virre (PRH Trade Register Information Service)](https://virre.prh.fi/) — Finnish Patent and Registration Office service: free basic data and free electronic trade register extract for companies and foundations; search a person's roles in companies and foundations by name and date of birth. Financial statements, articles of association and translated extracts are paid. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [PRH — Avoin data (PRH Open Data)](https://avoindata.prh.fi/fi) — free APIs for YTJ basic data of companies in the trade register, registered notices and digital financial statements. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Verohallinto — Yhteisöjen julkiset verotiedot (Tax Administration — Public Tax Data of Corporate Entities)](https://vero.fi/tietoa-verohallinnosta/verohallinnon_esittely/tietosuoja-ja-julkisuus/verotuksen_julkiset_tiedo/yhteisoasiakkaan_tuloverotuksen_julkise/) — taxable income, total tax, residual tax and refunds of companies and other entities, published as open data (CSV); data for tax year 2025 become public on 12 Nov 2026. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Finder.fi](https://www.finder.fi/) — company and decision-maker search by Fonecta: contacts, company data, decision-makers and financial figures. *Lang: FI.*
- [Kauppalehti — Yrityshaku (Company Search)](https://www.kauppalehti.fi/yritykset) — business daily's company search: revenue, financial data and contacts by name, business ID or industry. *Lang: FI.*
- [North Data](https://www.northdata.com/) — North Data covers company data in Finland as part of its XL package, which includes comprehensive information on companies, key persons, and financials. *Lang: EN, DE, FR.*
- [Tesari First Resort](https://tesari.ai/) — Tesari is an all-source intelligence platform designed for high-stakes environments, with applications ranging from trade compliance and supply chain illumination to financial crime investigations and complex litigation. It is a paid platform that offers a Tech for Good (Community Impact) Programme for OSINT practitioners and journalists. *Lang: EN.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [Karttapaikka (National Land Survey Map Service)](https://asiointi.maanmittauslaitos.fi/karttapaikka/) — Maanmittauslaitos map service with topographic, background and aerial-photo maps and property boundaries with property identifiers. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Paikkatietoikkuna (Finnish Geoportal)](https://www.paikkatietoikkuna.fi/) — national geoportal with map layers from many public authorities: properties, buildings, planning, environment, history. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Ryhti — Rakennetun ympäristön tietojärjestelmä (Built Environment Information System)](https://ryhti.syke.fi/) — national system combining land-use planning and building data, with a map service and open APIs. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Vanhatkartat.fi (Old Maps of Finland)](https://vanhatkartat.fi/) — about 30,000 map sheets from 1863–2020 based on National Land Survey maps; compare how places have changed. *Lang: FI.*
- [Kulttuuriympäristön palveluikkuna (Cultural Environment Service Window)](https://www.kyppi.fi/palveluikkuna/portti/read/asp/default.aspx) — Finnish Heritage Agency registers of archaeological sites and built cultural heritage, with a map. *Lang: FI.*
- [Helsingin karttapalvelu (Helsinki Map Service)](https://kartta.hel.fi/) — City of Helsinki web GIS: city maps, plans, property and building layers, aerial photos and 3D. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Asuntojen hintatiedot (Housing Price Information)](https://asuntojen.hintatiedot.fi/haku/) — rents and maintenance charges by area. The sales-price section has been closed because the data agreement with the Central Federation of Finnish Real Estate Agencies ended. *Lang: FI, SV.*

## Vehicles
### Land
- [Traficom — Tarkista ajoneuvon tiedot (Check Vehicle Data)](https://www.traficom.fi/fi/autoilijat/ajoneuvon-osto-ja-myynti/tarkista-ajoneuvon-tiedot) — free check of the technical and tax data of any registered vehicle by registration number or VIN. Owner and history data are paid. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Traficom — Tieto.Traficom (Transport Statistics)](https://tieto.traficom.fi/fi) — statistics and statistical database on vehicles, traffic and communications. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*

### Maritime
- [Traficom — Julkiset vesikulkuneuvotiedot (Public Watercraft Data)](https://www.traficom.fi/fi/veneily/veneen-rekisterointi/julkiset-vesikulkuneuvotiedot) — lookup by registration number of a registered boat: technical data, engine data, owner and holder names. Paid. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Digitraffic — Meriliikenne (Fintraffic Marine Traffic Open Data)](https://www.digitraffic.fi/meriliikenne/) — free real-time open data APIs: AIS vessel positions and metadata, port calls of merchant ships, winter-navigation icebreaker data. *Lang: FI, EN.*
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/) — live AIS vessel map with vessel and port database. Freemium: live map free; some features paid. *Lang: EN.*
- [Equasis](https://www.equasis.org/) — international database of ship and shipping-company safety information: IMO number, flag, owner, manager, inspections. Free registration required. *Lang: EN.*

### Aviation
- [Traficom — Julkiset ilma-alustiedot (Public Aircraft Data)](https://www.traficom.fi/fi/ilma-alukset-ja-lentokelpoisuus/ilma-alusten-rekisterointi-ja-kiinnitykset/julkiset-ilma-alustiedot) — free lookup by OH- registration of any registered aircraft: technical and airworthiness data, names of owner, holder, operator and representative (unless a disclosure restriction applies). *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/) — live and historical aircraft tracking without filtering of blocked aircraft; search by registration, ICAO hex or callsign. *Lang: EN.*

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [Fonecta Caller](https://www.fonecta.fi/) — largest Finnish directory: search persons by name or number, companies and services; names come from telecom operators' registers. Free account gives 10 person or number lookups a month; company results open without login. *Lang: FI.*
- [Eduskunta — Kansanedustajat (Parliament of Finland — MPs)](https://www.eduskunta.fi/kansanedustajat-ja-toimielimet) — MPs and parliamentary bodies; MPs' mandatory declarations of interests (sidonnaisuudet) are published on the Parliament website. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Vaali- ja puoluerahoitusvalvonta — Ilmoitukset henkilöittäin (Election Funding Disclosures by Person)](https://www.vaalirahoitusvalvonta.fi/fi/index/vaalirahoitus/haetietoavaalirahoitusilmoituksista/ilmoituksethenkiloittain.html) — National Audit Office register of candidates' campaign funding disclosures (donors and expenses), plus party funding reports. *Lang: FI, SV.*
- [Vaalien tulospalvelu (Election Results Service)](https://tulospalvelu.vaalit.fi/) — Ministry of Justice results of parliamentary, presidential, municipal, county and European elections by candidate, party and area. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [JulkiTerhikki (Public Register of Health Care Professionals)](https://julkiterhikki.lvv.fi/) — public search of the central register of health care professionals: right to practise and professional title; anyone can check a person's professional qualification. *Lang: FI, SV.*
- [Löydä asianajaja (Finnish Bar Association — Find a Lawyer)](https://app.loydaasianajaja.fi/) — search of members of the Finnish Bar Association (asianajaja title holders). *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [HisKi (Historical Parish Records)](https://hiski.genealogia.fi/) — Genealogical Society of Finland database of transcribed baptism, marriage, burial and migration records from parish books. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [Hilma (hankintailmoitukset.fi)](https://www.hankintailmoitukset.fi/) — national e-notification service for public procurement: contract notices and award notices of Finnish contracting authorities. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Tutkihankintoja.fi (Explore Procurement)](https://www.tutkihankintoja.fi/) — State Treasury and Hansel service showing what the state buys and from whom: purchases by agency, supplier and category. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [Markkinaoikeus — Hankinta-asiat (Market Court — Procurement Decisions)](https://www.markkinaoikeus.fi/ratkaisut/hankinta-asiat/) — published Market Court decisions in public procurement disputes. *Lang: FI, SV.*
- [Hansel](https://www.hansel.fi/) — central purchasing body of the Finnish state: framework agreements, dynamic purchasing systems and contract suppliers. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [KKV — Julkiset hankinnat (Competition and Consumer Authority — Public Procurement)](https://www.kkv.fi/tutkimus-ja-vaikuttaminen/julkiset-hankinnat/) — supervisor of procurement rules: supervision decisions, studies and guidance. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [TED — Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu/en/) — EU procurement-notice search; filter by country Finland for above-threshold notices and contract awards. *Lang: EN, multi.*

## WHOIS
- [domain.fi — Whois](https://registry.domain.fi/search/fi/App/Whois) — Traficom's official public data of .fi domains: status, registrar, name servers and holder data where public. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [domain.fi — Verkkotunnusvälittäjät (Registrar List)](https://registry.domain.fi/search/fi/App/registrars) — list of registrars of .fi domains; useful for resolving the registrar shown in WHOIS. *Lang: FI, SV, EN.*
- [IANA — .fi Delegation Record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/fi.html) — official delegation data for .fi: registry operator Traficom, contacts and name servers. *Lang: EN.*
- [RIPE Database Query](https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query) — RIPE NCC WHOIS for IP ranges and AS numbers allocated to Finnish networks. *Lang: EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records and subdomain discovery. *Lang: EN.*
